What it isThe website for a Kathmandu college teaching University of Wolverhampton degrees
Shown hereThe home page, 3 course pages and 5 inner pages
My roleUI/UX design
Home page

One giant word per section.

The home page is long, and it keeps you oriented with huge headings, each in its own colour. You always know which chapter you're in, even mid-scroll. Pick a word to jump the page to its section.

My reading of the colour per heading

The university heading reads UNIVERISTY in the design.

Teasers

Every teaser opens a page.

Most home sections are a short teaser with a "Learn more" or "More info" link. The inner pages in this set pick each one up and tell the full story. Pick a teaser to open the page it leads to.

Matched by title and breadcrumb

    Inner pages

    The home is colourful. The inside is lime.

    Once you leave the home page, every header follows the same recipe: a lime block with a capitalised headline, a photo that overlaps its corner, and a breadcrumb underneath. Five pages, one shape.

    The blockLime, with the headline in heavy capitals.
    The overlapA photo pushed into the block's lower right corner, so the header has depth without a hero image.
    The breadcrumbHome, then the page, underlined in lime.
    Courses

    One template, three degrees.

    The course page is built once and recoloured per degree: green for IT, blue for business, deep teal for the MBA. A badge carries the degree name, and a facts card sits beside the video. Switch degrees to compare.

    The facts are transcribed from each variant. Orange marks values that haven't been changed for the degree yet.

    Two generations

    Two ways to end a page.

    The pages end in one of two ways. The home, course and projects pages use a quiet question with Visit and Apply Now. The university, scholarship, exposure and infrastructure pages use a giant FORM over a faded photo. Each comes with its own footer.

    Looking back

    What I'd tighten next.

    The coloured headings and the lime template give the site a clear inside and outside. The work left is mostly content and picking one version.

    1. Pick one ending.Merge the two footers, keep the real contact details, and fix "INFRANSTRUCTURE" in both. "Pogramme" and "Univeristy" need the same pass.
    2. Give every course its own words.All three variants are headed Computer Science with the same UCAS code, the MBA is marked undergraduate, and the IT page has no Fees or Prospectus. Module text and the John Doe quote are filler.
    3. Match titles, breadcrumbs and photos.The infrastructure breadcrumb says Our Team, and "Learning Beyond Classrooms" says Make Learning Fun. The exposure and projects pages share most of their photos, and the scholarship artwork is cropped at its edges.
