The vehicle record

Three clocks on one car.

A vehicle's page keeps three separate histories under one tab bar: what the car did, what the system said about it, and what people changed. Keeping them apart stops a speeding alert being buried under a device update.

My reading of the Events, Notification and Activity tabs

Events

Read one morning.

The Events tab lists one car's morning on 31 December 2025, newest first. Laid out on a line, the same entries tell a story: left home, sped, parked, set off again.

Redrawn from the first eight entries in the Events list, oldest first and evenly spaced rather than to scale. Green marks when the car was running.

Customers

Tracking that can call for help.

The customer record goes further than a fleet tool needs to. Next to the vehicles, it keeps a safety-net contact, police mobilisations and a government document. The sidebar has a Police Request page too.

Interpretation: Locomo is a safety service as much as a tracker
  • Vehicles AssignedOne customer can own several vehicles, each with its identifier and plate.
  • Safety Net ContactSomeone to reach when something goes wrong.
  • Police MobilisationsA record of when the police were called out for this customer.
  • Subscription & InvoicesTracking is a paid service, billed per customer.
  • TripsJourneys across all of the customer's vehicles.
  • ActivityWho changed this record, and when.
Looking back

What I'd tighten next.

The fleet split, the three histories and the safety tabs are the ideas worth keeping. These are the next fixes.

  1. Darken the action green.White text on the bright button green comes out around 2.3:1, under the 4.5:1 that small text needs. A deeper green would keep the brand and pass.
  2. Fill in the pictures.Vehicle photos and the colour cards in Add Vehicle are still grey boxes. A picker that chooses by colour needs to show the colour.
  3. Quiet the delete buttons.A customer's vehicle list shows a solid red Delete on every row. An overflow menu with a confirm step would make the dangerous action harder to hit by mistake.
