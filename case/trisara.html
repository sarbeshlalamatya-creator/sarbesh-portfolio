Plan and final

Below the hero, a different site.

The plan was a long, dark scroll. Four themed sections each paired one interior photo with a heading (Fine Ambience, Exclusive Atmosphere, Attention at Eye-Level, Unique Piece of Art), with an outlets slider in the middle. The final page drops all of it for one bright wall of the group's brands, and each outlet gets its own card.

The change came out of joint discussion with the client. I'm showing what changed, not guessing at every reason.

Read from the two home pages.

The mosaic

Four names, one wall.

The final body is a single strip of six columns. Logos and one-line pitches sit between tall photos, so the eye reads brand, room, brand, room. Pick a brand to read its line as it appears on the wall.

Outlet cards

The neighbours wait at the edges.

Each outlet gets a full-width photo with a white card cut into one corner, holding its logo, a paragraph and Learn More. Thin tabs on the edges carry the next outlet's name, so the row reads as a carousel without arrows. Step through the three.

My reading of the edge tabs

Looking back

What I'd tighten next.

The final page trades atmosphere for clarity, and the outlet cards keep some of the mood the plan was after. A few loose ends made it through.

  1. Finish the hero sentence.Both versions end mid-word: "From the aroma of freshly baked go". It's the first paragraph anyone reads.
  2. Give each tile its own words.Both Baking Factory tiles reuse the Peros Pizza line, and the mosaic repeats the same photos across columns.
  3. Bring back the hours.The final footer dropped opening hours. The Trisara card says 11am till midnight, and the planned footer said something else, so it's worth settling in one place.
