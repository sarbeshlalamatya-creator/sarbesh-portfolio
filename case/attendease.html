AttendEase

Student Attendance Portal

Where do I need to be, and am I keeping up?

What it isThe student side of AttendEase: class routine and attendance record
Shown here6 screens: routine and attendance, on desktop and phone
My roleUI/UX design
The idea

Two tabs, two questions.

The admin side of AttendEase has nineteen pages in its menu. The student side has two buttons. Each one answers a question a student actually asks, and nothing else gets in the way.

My reading of the two-tab layout
RoutineWhere do I need to be?Today's classes with room, block, teacher and time, or the whole week at a glance.
AttendanceAm I keeping up?One percentage per module, the counts behind it, and every session that made it.
Daily routine

The rail tells you before you read.

Each class hangs off a coloured rail with a marker. Green with a tick is done, blue half-filled is happening now, grey is still to come. A cancelled class is struck through on pink, and a substitute is called out on amber.

Weekly routine

Colour is the module. The chip is the class type.

The weekly view stacks six days of cards. Each module keeps one stripe colour all week, and a small chip says whether it's a lecture, tutorial or lab. Pick a module to see where it falls.

Rebuilt from the weekly screen, 08 to 13 Dec. The amber card is a substitute class.

Attendance

Late still counts.

The module summary reads 83% from 23 present, 1 late and 5 absent out of 29. That only adds up if the late class counts: 24 of 29 is 82.8%, while 23 of 29 would be 79%. The number on screen quietly answers the question students worry about most.

Worked out from the figures on the screen
83%

23 present + 1 late = 24 of 29

Nine ways a session can end.

Each recent session carries a status badge. Present and late are only the start: a class can be cancelled, swapped for an exam, moved to another group or fall on a holiday. On a phone, the longer ones shrink to initials.

Responsive

Same content, rearranged for a thumb.

On a phone, Routine and Attendance become two full-width buttons. The summary bar folds into a ring and four tiles, session rows stack, and the weekly grid turns into a list of days you can open, each with a class count.

Looking back

What I'd tighten next.

Two tabs, the status rail and the module colours keep the student side light. These are the next fixes.

  1. Spell out the badges.On a phone, "CC", "ES", "LGA" and "GC" replace the full status. A student shouldn't need the info icon to learn they have an exam. Two lines, or a shorter word, would fit.
  2. One clock.The daily routine mixes 02:00, 04:00 and 17:00. Without AM or PM, the afternoon classes read as if they're in the middle of the night. 24-hour times throughout would settle it.
  3. Give red fewer jobs.Absent, Class Canceled, Exam Scheduled and Group Changed all use a red outline. Only Absent is the student's problem; the others are news, and could use a neutral or blue style.
