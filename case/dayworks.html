Dayworks.

Day-work app for Japan

An app that says すぐ, right away, before it shows a single job.

What it isA mobile app for picking up single shifts, working them and getting paid
Shown here5 onboarding slides and 3 app screens, in Japanese
My roleUI/UX design
Onboarding

Five slides, one yellow word each.

Every slide has the same build: a white line, a flat icon with three ticks either side, and the promise in yellow. Three of the four promises are about speed. Tap through the slides as they work in the comp.

The English lines are my translation
  1. すぐ働いて すぐお金がもらえる

    Work right away, get paid right away.

  2. 応募したら 即確定

    Apply and the job is yours on the spot. A small note adds that some jobs are exceptions.

  3. 働いたら すぐお金を受け取れる

    Once you've worked, collect your pay right away.

  4. レビューも見れて 安心

    You can read reviews too, so you can relax.

  5. さあ、はじめよう

    Let's get started. The button changes from Next to Start.

Promise and proof

Where each promise is kept.

Onboarding makes four promises. Each one has a place in the app screens that backs it up. Pick a promise to see where.

The pairings are my reading

Home

A week strip, not a search box.

Home opens on a date, not a query. A week runs across the top with today circled, past days greyed, Sunday in red and Saturday in blue. Under it a switch narrows the list to jobs still taking people. I rebuilt it in Japanese so the day picker and the switch work.

The price reads both ways.

Job detail

Everything before you say yes.

Applying confirms the job at once, so the detail page has to answer every question first. It runs from photo and pay through conditions, what to bring, a full overview, a map, pay rules and the company's reviews. Pick a part to jump to it.

Looking back

What I'd tighten next.

The onboarding sets up a clear promise and the three app screens follow through on it. These are the gaps I'd close.

  1. Show closed jobs on the card.The Shinjuku card on Home looks open, but its detail page says 締切 and greys out the button. A closed tag on the card would save a tap.
  2. Say how fast pay is.The pay table says month-end close and payment on the 15th of the next month. Pay "right away" depends on the prepayment tab, and a line on the job page could say so.
  3. Lift the quiet parts and swap the test data.The pale label on the white Next button and the active dot sit close to their backgrounds. Test company names, a test station and "Test" reviews are still in the comps.
