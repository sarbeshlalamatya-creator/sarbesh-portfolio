My roleUI/UX design
The idea

Four verbs, in order.

The sign-up screen carries the app's tagline: Discover. Watch. Book. Earn. I read it as the whole product in four words, and the screens line up behind it one verb at a time.

My reading of the tagline

Watch

The feed is the listing.

Hippie Clips looks like a short-video feed, but every clip carries what a search result would: the kind of place, a rating, how far it is from Kathmandu, a price and a Book Now button.

Interpretation: a video feed doing the job of search results
      Book

      Five steps, one stretching pill.

      From "Check Dates & Prices" the booking runs dates, package, guests, payment, then confirmation. A row of dots sits top right, and the current step stretches into a pill so you always know how far there is to go.

      Earn

      Three slides, three promises.

      Onboarding doesn't explain buttons. It makes one promise per slide, and each promise is something the rest of the app has to keep.

      Host

      The other side of the calendar.

      Owners get their own tabs: Reservations, Events, Trekking and Dashboard. The month grid a guest uses to pick dates comes back three times, each time with a different job.

      One component, reused across both sides
      AvailableBookedMaintenanceBlocked

      A front desk in a pocket.

      The dashboard opens on today: rooms occupied, guests checking in, money taken. Below that, New Booking, Check-In, Restaurant and Reports, then the day's arrivals and departures by name.

      Names, rooms and revenue figures are sample content in the design.

      Looking back

      What I'd tighten next.

      The four verbs give the app a clear spine, and the calendar ties the two sides together. These are the gaps I'd close next.

      1. Pick one icon language.Emoji such as 📅, 👥 and 💰 sit beside line icons, sometimes on the same screen. One drawn icon set would look calmer and render the same on every phone.
      2. Lift the green buttons.White text on #4CAF50 comes out around 2.8:1, below the 4.5:1 that small text needs. A deeper green or dark text would fix every primary button at once.
      3. Close the Earn loop.Onboarding promises a 10% commission, but none of these screens show how a guest posts a clip or tracks what it earned. That journey is next to design.
