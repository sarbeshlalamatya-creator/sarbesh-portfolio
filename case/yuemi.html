Bestsellers

Tiles cut to the shape of the product.

Seven categories sit in one bento grid on warm gold. The tall tile holds a headlight bulb, the long one holds a car, and the boxy one holds an amplifier. Each object gets the frame it fits. Pick a tile to see what it holds.

My reading of the tile sizes

Product rows

Two shelves, the same three boxes.

The product cards keep it plain: the maker's poster, the full model name, the price in rupees. The posters bring their own styles, dark, pale and grey, and the frame holds them together. Switch between the two rows below.

Promo band

Where the picture and the product agree.

Halfway down, a gold band sells one thing: a car multimedia player with CarPlay and Android Auto. The headline, the product and the gold all line up. It reads like the hero this store could have opened with.

My reading
Colour

Orange is the brand. Three more colours do the work.

The logo and the swooshes are orange. The buttons are blue, the active tab and Subscribe are green, and the stars and footer name are yellow. Pick a colour to see its jobs.

Looking back

What I'd tighten next.

The bento grid and the product posters already show what the store is for. These fixes would make the rest of the page agree.

  1. Open with the car.Lead with the dash cam or the multimedia player, and bring the footer's product list in line with the seven real categories.
  2. Make each row earn its place.Featured and Best Seller show the same three products. The Car Infotainment tab shows cameras. Two rows of different products, filtered by the tab, would double what the page shows.
  3. Fewer accents, readable text.Blue, green and yellow could become orange and ink. The hero's line "Empowering your aspirations with cutting-edge solutions" is white on a pale background, and it needs a darker colour.
