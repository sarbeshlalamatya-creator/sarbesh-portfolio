The family

Three colleges, three accents.

ICP sits beside Herald and IIC in the ING group, and shares their page types and the graduates photo at the end of every page. Each site gets one accent of its own. Herald has the lime block, IIC has the white card on a photo, and ICP has a dark header with the photo fading in from the right.

Home

Eight circles, eight jobs.

The home page uses circles the way Herald uses colour. Some hold a heading, some crop a photo, some hang off the page edge. I counted eight. Pick one to jump to it on the page.

The jobs are my reading of the layout

Inner pages

Six headers, one paragraph.

Every inner page opens the same way: a dark band, the page name in heavy white capitals, a photo fading in on the right. The photo changes with the page. The paragraph under the title doesn't.

1/6Informatics College Pokhara, established in 2011 is an associate college of ING. Initially, it started its academic journey as a franchisee of Informatics Education Limited, Singapore.

BBA page

One module card, three states.

The modules section is drawn as a state sheet: one card closed, one opened with its chevron flipped, one filled red. I've rebuilt it so the states work. Hover or focus a card for the red, click to open it. The comp repeats one module on every card, and so does the rebuild.

Red as the hover state is my reading
Life at ICP

Each column starts a step lower.

The Life at ICP page sets five event photos in three columns, each one dropped a little further than the last, so the grid reads as a slope. One card, Holi, shows its caption over the photo. Switch the step off to see what it adds.

Infrastructure

Five rooms, five sentences.

On IIC, six rooms shared one cafeteria sentence. Here each room gets its own, set in red capitals beside a zigzag of photos. Pick a room.

Written for this room
News

From the card to the article.

The news list is a plain three-column grid with Events and Blog tags. The article page is the richer one: a white card with a curved top laid over the banner, a mixed photo grid, and a Similar Posts row that runs off the right edge. Scroll both.

A carousel behind the cut-off row is my guess
Looking back

What I'd fill in next.

ICP gets two things right that IIC didn't: the footer is its own, Matepani-12 in Pokhara, and every room has its own sentence. What's left is copy carried over from the sister sites and a few placeholders.

  1. Take the sister colleges' names out.The courses and modules intros end with "Herald College Kathmandu (HCK)". The news cards and the article byline say Itahari International College. The article is titled "Islington Holi 2023" over a Spring Carnival banner and IIC Fiesta 2022 photos. The scholarship text thanks "the whole Herald family", and one criterion names Islington College and Herald College Kathmandu.
  2. Replace the placeholders.Lorem ipsum in the scholarship blocks and the news intro, "[[NAME]]" for the CEO twice, every BBA module card reading Software Project Management, IT careers on a business page, and a recipients list that repeats seven names.
  3. Match the labels.The BBA page heading says Digital Business Management while its badge says International Business. Every header has the same paragraph, and Infrastructure appears twice in the footer links.
