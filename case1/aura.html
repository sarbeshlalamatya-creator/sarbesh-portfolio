The name

One shop, three names.

The logo says aura. The account form, the footer and the house label say CELAVI. Parts of the home page still carry ASOS campaigns and brand tiles, so I've read this as a rebrand built on a large retailer's pattern, not a layout drawn from scratch. Pick a name to see where it shows up.

My reading, from the names on screen
Looking back

What I'd tighten next.

The journey is complete, and each screen knows its job. What's left is making it one shop, in one place.

  1. Pick one name.aura, CELAVI and ASOS all appear. One name across the logo, the account form, the footer and the campaigns would stop a shopper wondering which store they're in.
  2. Sell to Nepal in Nepal's terms.The flag says Nepal, but prices are in pounds, the membership is "Premier UK", and the product page promises free delivery that the bag then charges £10 for. Rupees, and one delivery story, would fix most of it.
  3. Match the states to what's possible.Move to bag shouldn't look active for a size that's out. The sale grid repeats the same eight products, and its footer says 72 viewed while 32 are shown.
